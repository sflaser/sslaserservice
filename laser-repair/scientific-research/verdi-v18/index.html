Current Service Scope

Verdi V18 work we can accept.

The following work is currently available. Inspection determines which work applies to your unit.

Available work

Five repair and adjustment services

  • Fault diagnosis and repair of the control-chassis power supply.
  • Frequency-doubling (SHG) crystal replacement.
  • Fault diagnosis and repair of the frequency-doubling crystal temperature-control circuit.
  • Pump laser diode (LD) parameter optimization and tuning.
  • Pump laser diode (LD) replacement.
Current limitation

Laser gain crystal replacement is unavailable

The frequency-doubling crystal and the laser gain crystal are different components. We can replace the frequency-doubling crystal; we cannot currently replace the laser gain crystal.

Assessment and Return

What happens after we receive the equipment.

Agree the inspection scope, applicable fees and shipping arrangements before sending the unit.

Initial assessment

Usually within 5 working days of receipt

We usually provide an initial assessment within 5 working days after receiving the equipment. More extensive testing, external coordination or spare-parts confirmation may take longer; we will communicate the revised timing. Repair completion timing is agreed separately.

If repair cannot proceed

Return after your confirmation

If repair is not feasible or the necessary conditions are not currently available, we will obtain your confirmation, restore the pre-inspection configuration and return the unit in its original packaging. Restoring this configuration does not mean restoring normal operating performance. Return shipping arrangements are agreed separately.
