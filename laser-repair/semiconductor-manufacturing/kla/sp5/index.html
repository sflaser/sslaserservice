Support Scope

What buyers usually need help with.

This inquiry covers the installed laser source and related components. It does not cover complete KLA inspection-tool servicing. Acceptance depends on the source configuration and fault details.

Common situations

Faults, drift, startup issues, output instability

Typical cases include unstable output, start-up problems, alarm behavior, laser-side failure checks, and replacement planning when the installed source or subsystem is suspected.

Best materials to send

Labels, fault notes, and equipment photos

Visible model labels, a short failure summary, alarm or interlock behavior, and photos of the source section all help move the review faster.

Evaluation Focus

What the review usually covers for SP5.

Start with the SP5 tool model and confirm the installed laser source separately. Share existing fault records and service history so the scope can be reviewed before any repair or replacement decision.

Platform confirmation

Confirm the installed unit and visible configuration

Start from the equipment name, visible labels, installed position, and any recent service history so the review is tied to the correct platform from the beginning.

Failure review

Check symptoms, alarms, and source-side behavior

Existing alarms and output records help establish whether the request concerns the laser source. A tool alarm alone does not identify a failed component; faults outside the source may require the equipment service provider.

Next-step decision

Decide repair, replacement, or parts support

After reviewing the source identity and available records, the next step may be a request for more information or a scoped repair, replacement or parts assessment. Compatibility and service availability must be confirmed before quoting.

Before You Inquire

What changed in your SP5 laser source?

Describe what you observed. Reduced output or an alarm does not by itself establish pump-diode, crystal or electronics damage.

Observed behavior

Output decline, startup changes or alarms

Share when the change began, whether it is intermittent, and any output or alarm records already available.

Source identity

Confirm the installed laser label

Send a clear label photo if one is already available. Do not select a replacement source based on the SP5 tool name alone.

Useful context

Fault history and current condition

Include recent service or replacement history and whether the source is still operating. Existing records are enough to start; additional checks can be discussed after review.
