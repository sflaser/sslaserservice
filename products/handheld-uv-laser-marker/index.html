Before ordering

Equipment questions.

Can I request a material test before choosing equipment?

Yes. Send the material, artwork and target result through our sample assessment page. We will review the request and confirm whether a test can be arranged, together with the sample requirements, cost and timing.

What should I include in an equipment inquiry?

Include the material and surface condition, workpiece size, marking artwork, quantity or expected throughput, delivery country and any required accessories. We can then confirm the proposed configuration and quotation details.

Does a marking inquiry also cover glass cutting?

This page covers surface marking. Glass cutting needs a separate process assessment with material, thickness and drawing details; a successful surface mark does not establish cutting performance.

What operating arrangements should I plan for?

Confirm the workspace, laser protection, ventilation and operator training needed for the actual application before use. The supplied configuration and operating instructions should be agreed during equipment selection.
