Sample assessment

Show us what a good mark looks like.

A photo of the workpiece and a reference finish make the first discussion more useful. Glass surface text marking is one application to assess; cutting, drilling and subsurface engraving require a different review.

  • Material type, grade and any surface coating
  • Workpiece size and flat or curved marking area
  • Artwork, mark size, contrast and durability targets
  • Sample quantity and acceptable marking location

Before sending samples

A few practical questions.

Should I ship my workpiece with the first inquiry?

Please send the details first. Wait until the sample requirements, receiving address, handling arrangements and return plan have been agreed.

Is every material or batch accepted?

Acceptance depends on material, surface, geometry and the requested result. The first review confirms whether a suitable test or processing route is available.

Can you quote before I have a finished drawing?

Send the information available, including photos or a sketch, material details, approximate quantities and the desired result. We can identify what else is needed for a useful quotation.
